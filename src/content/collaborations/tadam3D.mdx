I had the privilege of exploring my long-standing interest in the physics of light, optics, and materials by participating in a hands-on project week with the **TADAM3D-µP** team. 

This project was a fantastic trilateral collaboration between Technische Universität Dresden, Wrocław University of Science and Technology, and the University of Zilina. By bringing together diverse expertise and state-of-the-art facilities, the initiative pushes the boundaries of micro- and nano-fabrication.

During the project week, I was actively involved in hands-on experimental workflows, which included:
* **Digital Design:** Developing CAD models specifically tailored for micro-scale additive manufacturing.
* **Two-Photon Polymerization (TPP):** Operating a Nanoscribe printer to fabricate incredibly complex microstructures and nanostructures.
* **Microscopic Analysis:** Moving beyond the naked eye to observe and analyze our printed structures using advanced electron microscopy—a definitive highlight of the project.

This experience was a perfect blend of science, creativity, and cross-border teamwork. A heartfelt thanks goes out to the entire TADAM team, the mentors, and our hosts at the University of Zilina. I am also incredibly grateful to Technische Universität Dresden and Leonardo Büro Sachsen for providing the funding and grant that made this collaboration possible.
