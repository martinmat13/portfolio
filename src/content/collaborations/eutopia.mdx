I participated in **EUTOPIA TeamWork**, an intensive four-week online international experience program. Working entirely remotely, I collaborated within a diverse, cross-cultural team of students from EUTOPIA-affiliated universities to solve a real-world organizational challenge.

Our specific project was partnered with **Legrand Care**, focused on the objective: *Improving Onboarding and Cross-Country Collaboration*. 

Through active collaboration and problem-solving, our team developed strategic initiatives targeting three core areas:

* **Employer Branding & Recruitment:** Formulating strategies to strengthen Legrand Care’s presence in the job market and effectively connect with young professionals who align with the company's core values.
* **Onboarding Optimization:** Proposing improvements to the onboarding process to ensure a seamless, welcoming transition for new hires entering the company.
* **Cross-Border Synergy:** Fostering a highly collaborative environment for international teams. We developed frameworks to integrate new colleagues effectively, ensuring they immediately felt like part of a cohesive, global team despite geographic boundaries.
