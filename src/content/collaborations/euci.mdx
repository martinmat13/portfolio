I completed the **EUTOPIA Certificate of Internationalisation (EUCI)** program, culminating in the publication of a comprehensive Reflective Portfolio. This portfolio captures how my global academic journey—including studying in Germany and my Erasmus exchange in Sweden—has shaped my technical learning, cultural mindset, and future ambitions.

Through this structured process, I developed and formally recognized several core competencies:

* **Intercultural Communication & Collaboration:** Navigating and facilitating effective communication across distinct cultural and professional backgrounds.
* **Reflective Growth:** Actively connecting theoretical academic concepts to practical, global contexts.
* **Diverse Teamwork:** Building synergy and driving results within highly diverse, international environments.
* **Global Ambition:** Solidifying my drive to build an international career in computational engineering and fluid dynamics.

I am proud to share this portfolio with my network, with the hope that it inspires other students to step outside their comfort zones and explore academic and professional opportunities beyond borders.
