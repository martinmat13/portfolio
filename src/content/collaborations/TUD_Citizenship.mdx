I successfully completed the **TUD Global Citizenship Lab**, the mandatory two-day introductory event for the Knowledge module of the TUD Global Citizenship Certificate (GCC) program. 

Beyond cognitive knowledge acquisition on various global topics, the lab emphasized collaborative exchange, practical experimentation, and cross-disciplinary learning between participants and experts. Key focus areas included:

* **Social-Emotional Learning:** Fostering group exchange and forming authentic emotional connections within both professional and private contexts.
* **Practical Sustainability & Well-being:** Engaging in educational modules centered on mental health awareness, climate protection, and sustainable horticulture.
* **Personal Development:** Developing an actionable, personal roadmap toward becoming a global citizen, supported by a committed community of like-minded peers and experts.
