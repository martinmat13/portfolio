I served as a Student Volunteer and Conference Assistant for the **DGMK/ÖGEW/SCI Conference 'C1 Building Blocks for Future Chemistry'**, held in Dresden, Germany. 

Working alongside the event organizers at DGMK e.V., my technical and organizational duties throughout the three-day event included:

* **On-site Preparation:** Setting up the conference desk, organizing materials, and preparing the poster session area.
* **Participant Management:** Assisting at the main conference desk and handling the registration of attendees.
* **Event Logistics:** Providing active assistance within the conference hall to ensure scheduled scientific sessions ran smoothly.
* **Speaker & Chair Support:** Offering direct service and care for guest speakers and session chairs to facilitate seamless presentations.
