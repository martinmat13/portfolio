Welcome to my Erasmus journey! This chapter of my life took me from TU Dresden to Sweden's thriving maritime hub, Gothenburg. Driven by my passion for marine engineering and computational science, I sought out Chalmers University of Technology to blend practical engineering with advanced modeling. 

I was incredibly fortunate to complete my master's thesis on hydrofoil control systems under the guidance of Professor Arash. But this exchange was about much more than just CFD simulations and PID controllers. From the scenic ferry arrival in Sweden to exploring the city, shopping at Willys, navigating local challenges, and even a unique meeting with the Mayor at Brunnsparken, this experience was a life-changing immersion into a new culture. 

To read the full details about my accommodation, travel experiences, and everyday life in Sweden, check out my dedicated travelogue.
