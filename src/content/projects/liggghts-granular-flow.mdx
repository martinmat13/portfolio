## Motivation

Rotating drums transition between slumping, rolling, cascading, and
centrifuging regimes as rotation speed increases. This project uses DEM to
predict the transition boundaries and compare avalanche frequency against
published experimental data.

## Methodology

Particle-particle and particle-wall contacts use a Hertz-Mindlin
no-slip contact model. The dimensionless Froude number

$$
Fr = \frac{\Omega^2 R}{g}
$$

is used to classify the flow regime, where $\Omega$ is the drum's angular
velocity and $R$ its radius.
