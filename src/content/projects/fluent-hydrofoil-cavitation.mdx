## Motivation

Cavitation inception on lifting surfaces limits the operating envelope of
marine propellers and hydrofoils. This study predicts the inception angle
of attack for a NACA 66 section using a homogeneous mixture multiphase
model.

## Methodology

The cavitation number

$$
\sigma = \frac{p_\infty - p_v}{\tfrac{1}{2}\rho U_\infty^2}
$$

was held constant across the angle-of-attack sweep by adjusting the
domain's reference pressure. Vapour fraction was tracked using the
Schnerr–Sauer model, with mesh refinement concentrated on the suction
surface where local pressure was expected to fall below vapour pressure
first.

## Results

Inception angle predictions matched published cavitation-tunnel data to
within 0.6°, with the model correctly capturing the shift from leading-edge
to mid-chord cavitation as angle of attack increased.
